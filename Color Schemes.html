Color Schemes

Every single time you use a color you're making a Color Scheme.

The most common would be:

  • Red, Yellow, Blue (Primary colors)
  • Green, Violet, Orange (Secondary colors)
  • Red orange,Yellow orange, Yellow green, Blue green, Blue purple, and Red purple. (Tertiary Colors)

More into how our colors can come together

For one we can start with color families including:

  1. Complementary
  2. Analogous
  3. Monochromatic

    4. There are others that act as branches from these three but they arent needed as much.

    A good place to find these branches would be Adobe color-wheel

    Complementary

    These are colors that are across from each other in the color wheel such as:

    • Orange and Blue
    • Red and Green
    • Yellow and Violet

    Analogous

    Meaning colors that are neighbors to eachother:

    • Red, Red orange, and orange
    • Green, Yellow green, and Yellow
    • Blue, Blue violet, and Violet

    A cool website to get color palletes is Coolors it can come in handy and is fun to use.

    Monochromatic

    A color with it's tints and shades. Meaning the color either being mixed with black or white.
